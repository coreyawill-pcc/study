How this was made

  1. Scanned two walls meeting at a corner with the 3d Scanner App in LiDAR mode.
  2. Processed the scan in Agisoft Metashape and exported it as a GLB model.
  3. Hosted the model on GitHub Pages in a web 3D viewer.
